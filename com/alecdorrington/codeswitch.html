object Codeswitch extends BrowserExtension[Storage & UnlimitedStorage]

Teaches you a language by translating a few words of every page you read. Each word of a page in a language that the reader reads fluently has a chance, the intensity that they choose, of being shown in one of the languages they are learning, highlighted, with the original word shown above it on hovering over it. In a page in a language being learnt, the words a level above the reader's are highlighted, with their meanings shown above them, and harder words are shown as their meanings, in the reader's own language.
