def merged(kept: Option[Lexeme], found: Lexeme): Lexeme

found, a lexeme as a source now gives it, merged into kept, the same lexeme as kept before, if any, in its order: without what the reader or a review took out of it (see Editing), each of its claims that the source gave before replaced by the claim that continues it, keeping what other sources, models and the reader put within it, as each translation of a text still given alike, or left out if none does; what the source gives that continues none after; and the rest kept as it was.
