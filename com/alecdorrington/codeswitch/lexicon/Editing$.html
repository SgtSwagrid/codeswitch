Editing

com.alecdorrington.codeswitch.lexicon.Editing
object Editing

How the reader, or a model in their place, changes a lexeme: by taking pieces out of it, by putting others in their place, and by undoing a change. Each piece taken out is kept as Rejected, so that nothing a source gave is lost, and a source that gives it again does not bring it back.
