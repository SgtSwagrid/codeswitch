final class FamiliarityService(storage: Storage, learning: IO[List[Learnt]])

The one source of how well the reader knows each word, kept in storage: as they rated it, or else as its difficulty for them puts it (see models.Familiarity.default), the two alike to all that reads them, and of the sum of all of them, for normalising them (see total). learning gives the languages being learnt, each with the reader's level, against which a word's difficulty is measured.

The one source of how well the reader knows each word, kept in storage: as they rated it, or else as its difficulty for them puts it (see models.Familiarity.default), the two alike to all that reads them, and of the sum of all of them, for normalising them (see total). learning gives the languages being learnt, each with the reader's level, against which a word's difficulty is measured.

Any part may read, but only the background writes, as it answers the rate and seen messages, one write at a time, so that the sum, of every word, kept with each write, is never lost to a write in another tab.

final class FrequencyService(storage: Storage)

The one source of how often the reader has met each word of a language being learnt on the pages they read, kept in storage, 0 for a word never met, of the total of each language (see models.FrequencyTotal), and of the word that each word of their own language was last switched into.

The one source of how often the reader has met each word of a language being learnt on the pages they read, kept in storage, 0 for a word never met, of the total of each language (see models.FrequencyTotal), and of the word that each word of their own language was last switched into.

Any part may read, but only the background writes, as it answers the hints and translate messages, one count at a time, so that two tabs counting a word at once lose neither, and each total stays the sum of its words' counts.
