The one source of how well the reader knows each word, kept in storage: as they rated it, or else as its difficulty for them puts it (see models.Familiarity.default), the two alike to all that reads them, moved by what they did with it on pages since (see models.Glance), and of the sum of all of them, for normalising them (see total). learning gives the languages being learnt, each with the reader's level, against which a word's difficulty is measured.

final class Scheduler

Runs programs that ask a model at most lanes at once, across every tab and the lexicon, the most urgent first, and those as urgent in the order they were asked for, so that what the reader waits on is not kept waiting behind pages, nor the page they read behind those they do not, and the provider is not asked more at once than its limits bear. A program cancelled while it waits leaves the queue, as when its page is restored for another pass.
