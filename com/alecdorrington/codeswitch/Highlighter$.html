Highlighter

com.alecdorrington.codeswitch.Highlighter
object Highlighter

Finds the words of the page to translate, and shows the pieces of their translations, each highlighted, where the language's word order puts it, with the card of its word shown by Tips on hovering over it. A translation whose draw is above the intensity is hidden, and reads as the original text, until the intensity rises above it. Annotated Text Format puts each into the page, and takes it out again, leaving the page's text as it was.
