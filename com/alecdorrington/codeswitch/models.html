enum Answer(val pull: Double, val label: String)

How well the reader answers that they knew a word, on a scale of five, from not at all to well, each with its pull, how far it moves the word's familiarity, as a share of Familiarity.step, down towards 0 if it is less than 0, or up towards 1 if more, and its label, what it means. Only knowing a word well raises it; knowing it mostly leaves it be.

val learnable: Localised[Language]

The languages a reader may learn or read in: every one, a broader language (German) as itself, but Chinese, which is written in two scripts, and German (Switzerland), which is both Swiss Standard German and Swiss German, only in one of their varieties, lest a page mix them. Each of those, chosen, stored or preferred by a browser (zh, de-CH), reads as its first, Simplified Chinese or Swiss Standard German. The pickers offer the listed languages, those two among them, each variety beneath its own.
