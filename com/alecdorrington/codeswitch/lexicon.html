final case class Pending(language: Language, spelling: Spelling, count: Double, opening: Boolean, since: Timestamp, hinted: List[Hinted] = ...)

A spelling of language read count times, perhaps in part, before it was looked up, first read at since, which waits to be looked up, so that it is counted toward the lexemes it is a form of once they are known. If it was read at the start of a sentence, opening, its capital may be only for that, as Die is die. A word that hints named, hinted, waits too, so that the spellings they were given for are listed among its forms once it is known (see services.Counting.heed).
