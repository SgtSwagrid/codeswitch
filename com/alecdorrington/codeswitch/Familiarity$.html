Familiarity

com.alecdorrington.codeswitch.Familiarity
object Familiarity

How well the reader knows each word above their level that they have rated, from 0, not at all, to 1, well, by the word's dictionary form, which decides how it is shown, in place of its level.

def key(language: Language, word: String): StorageKey[Option[Double]]

Where the familiarity of word, a dictionary form of language, is kept, if the reader has rated it. Each word has a key of its own, so that a rating in one tab is not lost to one in another, nor rewrites the rest. The word keeps its case, so that Weg, a path, is apart from weg, away, and the language, so that a word is apart from one spelt alike in another. The language is the whole one, not its variety, so that a word rated in German (Germany) is known in Swiss German too, and a language stored as a whole one, as before varieties, is rated and read under one key.

def kind(familiarity: Double): Kind
