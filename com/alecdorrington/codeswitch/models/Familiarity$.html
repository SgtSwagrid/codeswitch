Familiarity

com.alecdorrington.codeswitch.models.Familiarity
object Familiarity

How well the reader knows a word, from 0, not at all, to 1, well, which decides how it is shown, by its dictionary form: as they rated it, or else as its difficulty for them puts it.

def key(language: Language, word: String): StorageKey[Option[Double]]

Where the familiarity of word, a dictionary form of language, is kept, if the reader has rated it. Each word has a key of its own, so that a rating in one tab is not lost to one in another, nor rewrites the rest. The word keeps its case, so that Weg, a path, is apart from weg, away, and the language, so that a word is apart from one spelt alike in another. The language is the whole one, not its variety (see whole), so that a word rated in German (Germany) is known in Swiss Standard German too, but not in Swiss German, a language of its own, and a language stored as a whole one, as before varieties, is rated and read under one key.
