def of(relative: Double, familiarity: Double): Double

How much the reader should focus on learning a word met relative times as often as the average word of its language, that they know as well as familiarity says: hardly at all if they are likely to know it, however often they meet it, and otherwise the more, the more often they meet it, up to about as often as the average word, beyond which it matters little more, so that the commonest words, as articles, do not crowd out the rest.
