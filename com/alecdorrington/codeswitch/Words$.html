def choose(words: List[TextSpan], floor: Double, intensity: Double, random: () => Double): List[Drawn]

Each of words longer than one letter whose draw is below intensity, the share of the words to translate. Each word is drawn a number from 0 to 1, and translated while the intensity is above it. Words already drawn up to floor, and not chosen, are drawn from floor to 1, so that raising the intensity keeps the words already chosen. Each draw is from random.
