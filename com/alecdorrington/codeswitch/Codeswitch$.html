Codeswitch

com.alecdorrington.codeswitch.Codeswitch
object Codeswitch extends BrowserExtension[Storage]

Teaches you a language by translating a few words of every page you read. Each word of a page in another language has a chance, the intensity that the reader chooses, of being shown in the language that the reader is learning, highlighted, with the original word shown above it on hovering over it. In a page in that language, the words a level above the reader's are highlighted, with their meanings shown above them, and harder words are shown as their meanings, in the reader's own language.
