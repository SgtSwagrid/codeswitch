Codeswitch

com.alecdorrington.codeswitch.Codeswitch
object Codeswitch extends BrowserExtension[Storage]

Teaches you a language by translating a few words of every page you read. Each word of a page has a chance of Words.share of being shown in the language that the reader is learning, highlighted, with the original word shown above it on hovering over it.
