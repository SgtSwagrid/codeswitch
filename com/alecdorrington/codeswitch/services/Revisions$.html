Revisions

com.alecdorrington.codeswitch.services.Revisions
object Revisions

Changes the reader's lexicon as they edit it, in its editor: taking pieces out of a lexeme, rewording them, having a model write them again, and undoing each change (see Editing). Only the background uses it, through the editor's messages, which keeps the lexicon out of the pages.
