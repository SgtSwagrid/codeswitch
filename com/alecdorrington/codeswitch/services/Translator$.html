Translator

com.alecdorrington.codeswitch.services.Translator
object Translator

Translates words in their contexts with a large language model, and keeps the reader's choice of model. Only the background and the popup use it, which keeps Iris's models out of the pages.

final case class Reviewed(merges: List[Merged], unneeded: List[String])

What repeats in a dictionary's entry, and what it no longer needs. Most entries have neither.

What repeats in a dictionary's entry, and what it no longer needs. Most entries have neither.

Value parameters

merges

Each group of parts of the entry that say the same, or so nearly the same that they could be one, with the one part that takes their place. A part is in one group at most, and parts that differ in meaning are never merged.

unneeded

The ids of the parts that the entry no longer needs: a definition or an example that says nothing that another of its sense does not. Never every sense, nor a part of a group.
