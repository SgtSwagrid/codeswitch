Lexicon

com.alecdorrington.codeswitch.services.Lexicon
object Lexicon

Looks words up for the card in the reader's own lexicon, kept on this device for good: as a dictionary the reader has not turned off gives them, fetched the first time a word is looked up, its texts put into the reader's language by a model, or else as a model generates them, where no dictionary has the word. Only the background uses it, which keeps Iris out of the pages.

def entry(native: Language, lookup: Lookup, store: Store, storage: Storage, fetch: Fetch, ask: Ask, review: LexemeId => IO[Unit]): IO[Option[(LexemeId, Entry)]]

The entry for lookup, a word of a language being learnt, explained in native, the reader's own, as kept in store, of what the dictionaries that the reader has not turned off in storage give: Wiktionary's, from fetch, the first time the word is looked up, of the word's class, with the gloss that best fits its meaning, its texts put into the reader's language by a model if that is not English; or else, if no dictionary has the word, one that a model generates, as it does if the dictionaries have it only in other classes than the lookup's (see Lookup.fits), as a dictionary may lack one, unless the model finds it no word of that class either.

The entry for lookup, a word of a language being learnt, explained in native, the reader's own, as kept in store, of what the dictionaries that the reader has not turned off in storage give: Wiktionary's, from fetch, the first time the word is looked up, of the word's class, with the gloss that best fits its meaning, its texts put into the reader's language by a model if that is not English; or else, if no dictionary has the word, one that a model generates, as it does if the dictionaries have it only in other classes than the lookup's (see Lookup.fits), as a dictionary may lack one, unless the model finds it no word of that class either.

If a model cannot put Wiktionary's texts into the reader's language, as when they have given no key, they are given in English, and none is kept, so that they are put into it once it can. Each part of a lookup (fetching a word, putting its texts into the reader's language, generating one) asked for again while it is being found waits for it, rather than asking again (see Once), and a model is not asked again for a minute after it failed (see unlessFailed). A lexeme kept with what the reader, or a model, put in it is given to review once its record is fetched again (see Reviews).
