Dictionary

com.alecdorrington.codeswitch.Dictionary
object Dictionary

Looks words up for the card: in Wiktionary, through Kaikki, wherever it has them, and otherwise from a model, keeping what it finds on this device. Only the background uses it, which keeps Iris out of the pages.

Concrete methods

def entry(native: Language, lookup: Lookup, storage: Storage, fetch: Fetch, ask: Ask): IO[Option[Entry]]

The entry for lookup, a word of a language being learnt, explained in native, the reader's own, as kept in storage, or else as found, then kept: Wiktionary's, from fetch, of the word's part of speech, with the gloss that best fits its meaning, its senses put into the reader's language by a model if that is not English; or else, if Wiktionary has none, one that a model makes.

The entry for lookup, a word of a language being learnt, explained in native, the reader's own, as kept in storage, or else as found, then kept: Wiktionary's, from fetch, of the word's part of speech, with the gloss that best fits its meaning, its senses put into the reader's language by a model if that is not English; or else, if Wiktionary has none, one that a model makes.

If a model cannot put Wiktionary's entries into the reader's language, as when they have given no key, they are given in English, and not kept, so that they are put into it once it can. Each part of a lookup (fetching a word's entries, putting them into the reader's language, a model's making one) asked for again while it is being found waits for it, rather than asking again (see Once), and a model is not asked again for a minute after it failed (see unlessFailed).
