final class Tips

The tooltips of the page, of the translation and of the hint under the pointer, each the card of its word (see Cards): what it means there, the original that a translation stands for, a hint's meaning, or the original of a gloss, with a button for each models.Answer to whether the reader knew it, and what the dictionary has of it. Each is shown above the line under the pointer, or below it where there is no room above, in the page's top layer, where nothing in the page can clip or cover it, and stays open while the pointer goes from the word to it. Each passage's hints, but those the reader knows and those in links, are one stop of Tab, the left and right arrow keys moving among them, each showing its tooltip, whose buttons Tab moves on to; translations take no focus. A tap shows either, as hovering does, and a second tap on a word in a link follows the link.
